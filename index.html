Welcome To Sweet Treat Bakery

The name of the business is Sweet Treats. The bakery all started back in 2020 after the lockdown. It has one founder who used to sell cupcakes at school and later grew it into a global bakery brand.

The bakery's mission is to provide freshly baked pastries. When you bake, you always bake for a reason because you are giving it to people. You make it the best you can, and you make it with love and stir love into it and present it to people.

Our vision statement is to be a top choice for pastries in the heart of Braam, bringing students and young professionals together through freshly baked sweet moments while keeping the cozy feeling of a local kitchen.

Goals

Providing fresh baked goods to our customers and ensuring that they are affordable for everyone to enjoy with different varieties to choose from our menu.

We have a simple check out system, building trust with customers. We display appetizing photos of our baked goods on our website and social media platforms to entice customers to visit our bakery.

Values

We make sure that we use the finest ingredients in all our baked goods. We ensure that every baked good meets high standards of superior taste and quality.

Our innovation is to bring creativity to life through our sensational recipes. We are committed to providing great customer service to our customers and we look on building a unique relationship with our clientele.

Our Team

We have a team of bakers who are passionate about baking and creating delicious treats for our customers.

Our team is dedicated to providing the best customer service and ensuring that every customer leaves satisfied.

We believe in teamwork and collaboration, and we work together to create a positive and welcoming environment for our customers.
